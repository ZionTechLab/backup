White-label brand assets for this deployment.

Drop your files here and point src/config/brand.js at them:
  logo.svg         -> brand.logo        (topbar, drawer, login)
  favicon.png      -> brand.favicon     (browser tab)
  report-logo.png  -> brand.reportLogo  (report and voucher print header)

Also update public/manifest.json for the PWA app name and icons.

Leave brand.js paths blank to keep the default letter-mark branding.
