## The Four Settings

| Setting | What it changes |
|---|---|
| Mode | Light or dark appearance for the whole app |
| UI Style | The overall visual style used for components |
| Color Palette | The accent color scheme (several named palettes, plus a "Specialized Palettes" group for more specific looks) |
| Menu Contrast | How much the left drawer stands out from the workspace — Subtle, Distinct, or High Contrast (Dark) |

Every change applies immediately — no Save button, and no page reload. Your choices are remembered the next time you sign in.

## Quick Toggle

You don't need this page just to flip light/dark — the moon/sun icon in the top bar (see [User Menu](user-menu.md)) does that in one click from anywhere.
