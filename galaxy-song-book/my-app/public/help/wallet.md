## What You See

- **Available Balance** — what's usable right now.
- **Total Top-ups** — lifetime total of all top-up transactions.
- **This Month** — total topped up in the current calendar month.
- **Top-ups YTD** — year-to-date top-up total.
- **Last 12 Months Top-ups** — a bar chart of monthly top-up amounts.
- **My Payment Journal** — a running ledger showing every transaction with opening balance, credit/debit amount, and closing balance. Debits come from subscription charges, API usage, and other module deductions.

## Make Payment

The **Make Payment** button in the top right opens a dialog for making a payment from the wallet. This is still being built — the dialog currently just confirms it isn't wired up yet.

## Note

This screen is currently a prototype: the numbers you see are sample data, not pulled from your real transaction history yet.
