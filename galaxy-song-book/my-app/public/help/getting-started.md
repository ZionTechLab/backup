# Getting Started

## Logging In

Sign in with the username and password your administrator gave you. If you've forgotten your password, contact your administrator to reset it — there's no self-service reset yet.

## Layout

- **Left drawer** — your menu, organized by module. What you see here depends on your assigned role.
- **Top bar** — search, theme toggle, and your account menu (top right).
- **Main area** — the screen for whatever you've selected.

## Finding a Screen

Use the drawer on the left, or the search in the top bar to jump straight to a screen by name.

## Saving Your Work

Most forms have a **Save** button in the top right of the page. Records move through statuses (e.g. Draft → Approved) as they're actioned — you'll see a status badge once a record has been saved once.

## Getting Help

This Help section is reachable any time from your account menu (top right) → **Help**.
