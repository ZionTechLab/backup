## Account Details

A read-only summary of your own account: full name, username, email, role, and (where set) department, employee ID, and hire date. To change any of these, contact your administrator — there's no self-service edit here.

## Change Password

Enter your current password, then a new one twice to confirm. The new password must be at least 6 characters. You'll see a success or error message right on the form — no page reload needed.

## Two-Factor Authentication

Shows whether an authenticator app is linked to your account. If it says **Not enabled**, ask your administrator whether 2FA is required for your role.
