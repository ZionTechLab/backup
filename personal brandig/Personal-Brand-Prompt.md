# Personal Brand Strategy — Thilina Perera

**Context:** I'm 38, Lead Software Engineer at Hayleys Advantis (promoted Apr 2026). I'm building Galaxy ERP (multi-tenant ERP) and an HR BPO platform on the side. I have 22+ years in software engineering, MSc candidate at Wrexham University, and I run black-box security audits for enterprise domains. I'm from Negombo, Sri Lanka.

**Goal:** Position myself as a technical founder and thought leader — not an employee who codes, but a builder who sees systems end-to-end.

---

## Your Job

Build a 12-month personal brand strategy. Not generic \"post more on LinkedIn\" advice — a specific, executable playbook.

---

## 1. Brand Positioning

Define:
- My unique angle. What do I know that my peers don't?
- My voice. Direct. Technical. No corporate jargon. No \"passionate\" or \"rockstar.\"
- My audience. Who reads my content? Future clients? Future employees? Future investors?
- My \"proof points\" — concrete things I've done that establish credibility

---

## 2. Content Pillars (4-5)

What topics do I own? Don't just list categories — define the angle:

| Pillar | My Angle (not generic) |
|--------|----------------------|
| Security audits | Real findings. Redacted but real. What internal teams miss. |
| Building in public | Galaxy ERP journey. PostgreSQL migration. Mistakes and lessons. |
| Career growth | 22+ years. IT support → Lead SE. What actually works. |
| Sri Lanka tech | Building global products from Negombo. Not just outsourcing. |
| AI/ML | Practical AI. Not buzzwords. MSc-level rigor. |

For each pillar, draft 3 sample post headlines.

---

## 3. 90-Day Content Calendar

30 posts (3 per week). For each:
- Topic
- Pillar
- Format (story, how-to, hot take, case study, question)
- Specific hook (first line)

Don't leave any blank. All 30 filled.

---

## 4. LinkedIn Profile Optimization

Rewrite:
- **Headline** — current: \"Lead — Software Engineering @ Hayleys Advantis Limited\"
- **About section** — 2,600 character max. Tell a story. Not a resume.
- **Featured section** — what should go here? Portfolio link? Best post? Case study?
- **Banner image** — what should it show?

---

## 5. Content Formats

| Format | When to Use | Example |
|--------|------------|---------|
| Story | Personal experience, lessons learned | \"I audited a domain last week. Here's what I found.\" |
| Hot take | Challenge conventional wisdom | \"Your CSP header doesn't protect you. Here's why.\" |
| How-to | Actionable, technical | \"How to find mock auth in your own production bundle\" |
| Case study | Proof of work | Redacted audit findings with methodology |
| Question | Engagement, community | \"What's the worst security gap you've found in production?\" |
