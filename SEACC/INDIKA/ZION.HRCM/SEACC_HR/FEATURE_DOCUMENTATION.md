# SEACC HRCM - Feature Documentation

Welcome to the comprehensive feature documentation for the SEACC HRCM (Human Resource Capital Management) system. This document provides a non-technical overview of the capabilities and functionalities available within the application, designed to streamline HR operations, payroll validation, and workforce management.

## 1. Core Human Resource Management
Manage your organization's most valuable asset—your people—with a robust set of tools for structural and personal data management.

*   **Organizational Hierarchy**: Define and manage complex organizational structures including Companies, Branches, Departments, Divisions, Sections, and Sub-sections to reflect your real-world business setup.
*   **Employee Profiles**: Maintain detailed digital records for every employee, including personal details, contact information, education history, and professional skills.
*   **Employment Management**: Track employee status, job titles, designations, and recruitment types.
*   **Document Management**: Attach and store important documents digitally against employee records for easy retrieval.

## 2. Time & Attendance Management
Automate and simplify the tracking of working hours, shifts, and leaves to ensure accuracy and compliance.

*   **Shift & Roster Management**: Create flexible shift schedules and assign rosters to individuals or teams. Support for complex shift patterns.
*   **Automated Data Capture**: Seamlessly integrate with attendance devices (biometric/card readers) to synchronize check-in and check-out data automatically.
*   **Leave Management**: A complete workflow for leave administration:
    *   Define custom leave types (Casual, Medical, Annual, etc.).
    *   Employees can submit leave applications.
    *   Managers can review and approve/reject leave requests.
    *   Track leave balances and entitlements.
*   **Overtime (OT) Automation**: Automatically calculate overtime hours based on attendance data and business rules, with a built-in approval workflow.
*   **Gate Passes**: Issue and manage approval for employee gate passes (exit permits) during working hours.
*   **Attendance Processing**: Tools to process daily, weekly, and monthly attendance records, identifying trends like late arrivals or early departures.
*   **Manual Adjustments**: Authorized personnel can manually correct shift allocations or attendance records when exceptions occur.

## 3. Payroll Management
Ensure timely and accurate compensation with a fully integrated payroll module.

*   **Flexible Salary Structures**: configure various earnings, allowances, deductions, and reimbursements. Support for different payroll levels and grades.
*   **Payroll Processing**: Automated monthly payroll generation based on attendance data, leave records, and defined salary structures.
*   **Statutory Compliance**: Manage statutory deductions and contributions such as Tax (PAYE), EPF, and ETF with configurable tables.
*   **Payslip Generation**: Generate detailed and compliant payslips for all employees, itemizing all earnings and deductions.
*   **Bank Integration**: Manage bank and branch details to facilitate direct salary transfers.
*   **Salary Adjustments**: Handle ad-hoc salary adjustments, bonuses, or deductions efficiently.

## 4. Administration & Security
Control who has access to what ensuring data privacy and system integrity.

*   **User Management**: Create and manage system user accounts.
*   **Role-Based Access Control**: Define granular permissions to restrict access to sensitive data or specific modules based on user roles (e.g., HR Manager vs. Payroll Clerk).
*   **System Backups**: Built-in utilities to backup system data towards safety and recovery.
*   **Security Reporting**: Monitor system usage and sensitive modifications.

## 5. Employee Engagement & Welfare
Features designed to support the well-being and engagement of your workforce.

*   **Canteen Management**: Manage meal plans, rates, and canteen usage tracking.
*   **Greetings Scheduler**: Automated system to send greetings for employee birthdays and work anniversaries.

## 6. Reporting & Analytics
Make data-driven decisions with a comprehensive suite of reports.

*   **Extensive Report Library**: Access hundreds of pre-built reports covering all aspects of HR, Attendance, and Payroll.
*   **Control Panels**: Dashboard-like views for Daily and Monthly attendance supervision to get quick insights into workforce status.
