**Source:** Lecture notes

**Status:** Guide only — not an assignment

---

## Proposal Structure (9 Sections)

| # | Section | What to Include |
|---|---------|----------------|
| 1 | Title Page | Project title, student name, supervisor, institution, date |
| 2 | Introduction & Background | Topic overview, context, why now, gaps, scope |
| 3 | Problem Statement | "The problem is..." — specific, with evidence, who's affected |
| 4 | Aim & Objectives | 1 aim sentence + 3-5 SMART objectives |
| 5 | Literature Review | 5-10 sources, theories, gaps identified, link to your work |
| 6 | Methodology | Qualitative/Quantitative, data sources, tools, dev method, ethics, limitations |
| 7 | Gantt Chart | Visual timeline — 3-6 months, tasks per week |
| 8 | Expected Outcomes | What's produced, who benefits, measurable results |
| 9 | References | Harvard style, 8-10 sources minimum |

---

## SMART Objectives

| Letter | Meaning | Example |
|--------|---------|---------|
| S | Specific | "To develop a web-based..." |
| M | Measurable | "...attendance system" |
| A | Achievable | "using React and MySQL" |
| R | Realistic | "for 3 user types" |
| T | Time-bound | "within 12 weeks" |

---

## Methodology Checklist

- Research approach (Qualitative / Quantitative / Mixed)
- Data collection (Primary: surveys, interviews / Secondary: books, articles)
- Tools & technologies (Python, MySQL, Figma, etc.)
- Development method (Agile / Waterfall / Prototyping)
- Ethical considerations (consent, privacy, data security)
- Limitations (time, budget, access)

---

## Tips for Strong Proposal

| Do | Don't |
|----|------|
| Write clearly, avoid jargon | Vague language ("improve the system") |
| Be specific ("reduce errors by 30%") | Plan 5 major tasks in one week |
| Keep sections connected (objectives → methodology → outcomes) | Skip proofreading |
| 2,000-3,000 words (check course guidelines) | Use Wikipedia as main source |
| Harvard referencing | Miss the deadline |

---

## Common Weaknesses That Lower Marks

- Weak or outdated literature review
- Poor methodology justification
- Lack of evaluation criteria
- Overly descriptive — not critical
- Ignoring supervisor feedback
