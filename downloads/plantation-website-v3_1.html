About

142 planting seasons.

Founded as a single 90-hectare garden in 1884. Locally owned since 1972, listed since 1993, still run by people who walk the fields.

Vision

The plantation company buyers choose when provenance has to be provable.

Mission

Grow quality crop on well-managed land, with fair work and honest reporting.

Values

Care for the land. Care for the people on it. Tell the truth about the crop.
