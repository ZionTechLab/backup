**My Profile:**

- Currently employed (will operate evenings/weekends initially)
- Building Galaxy ERP on the side (multi-tenant ERP for manufacturing companies)

**Your Job:** Do what I can't — deep market analysis, competitive intelligence, financial modeling, strategy.

## 3. Financial Modeling

### Revenue Models (Compare)
| Model | How It Works | Pros | Cons |
|-------|-------------|------|------|
| Per-employee-per-month (PEPM) | e.g., LKR 500/employee | Predictable recurring | Low per-unit revenue |
| Flat monthly retainer | e.g., LKR 50K flat | Simple to sell | Mispriced for different sizes |
| Per-transaction | e.g., per payslip, per hire | Fair usage-based | Unpredictable |
| Tiered packages | Basic/Pro/Enterprise | Upsell path | Complexity |

Which model maximizes LTV while staying affordable for SMEs?

### Unit Economics
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) — LinkedIn ads? Referral? Cold outreach?
- Lifetime Value (LTV) — average retention for HR outsourcing?
- LTV:CAC ratio target
- Gross margins per service line (payroll vs recruitment vs compliance)

### 3-Year Financial Projection
- Year 1: Solo, part-time. Revenue, costs, profit.
- Year 2: Full-time, 1-2 employees. Revenue, costs, profit.
- Year 3: 5+ employees, PVT Ltd. Revenue, costs, profit.
- Include realistic assumptions (not hockey-stick fantasy)
