### Business Registration (Sri Lanka)
- **Sole Proprietorship:** Lowest barrier, minimal capital requirement — register at Divisional Secretariat
- **Private Limited Company:** Register via Department of Registrar of Companies (ROC)
- **Key documents:** Business name approval, Articles of Association, director/shareholder details
- **Minimum capital:** No statutory minimum for PVT Ltd (practically LKR 100,000+ recommended)
- **Tax registration:** Inland Revenue Department — TIN, VAT if turnover exceeds LKR 15M/year
- **EPF/ETF:** Mandatory if you hire employees — register with Labour Department

### Why HR BPO?
- Every company needs HR — payroll, compliance, recruitment, training
- SMEs can't afford full HR departments → outsource
- Tech-enabled HR BPO has higher margins than manual services
- Your edge: software engineering background → automate what competitors do manually

## How to Start (First 90 Days)

1. **Register company** — sole proprietorship or PVT Ltd
2. **Build website** — landing page, services, contact form
3. **Develop automation** — payroll calculator, leave tracker, CRM
4. **Target first 3 clients** — network through Hayleys contacts + LinkedIn
5. **Document everything** — SOPs, templates, compliance guides
6. **Scale** — hire first employee after 6+ clients
