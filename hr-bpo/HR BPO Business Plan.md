## Executive Summary

This proposal outlines the launch of a **tech-enabled HR Business Process Outsourcing (BPO)** company in Sri Lanka, leveraging Thilina Perera's 22+ years of software engineering expertise to deliver automated, cost-effective HR services to SMEs.

Sri Lanka's IT/BPO sector exports grew from **US$213 million (2007) to US$1.2 billion (2021)** — a 5.6x increase in 14 years. The country ranks among the **top 50 global outsourcing destinations** (AT Kearney), and Colombo is ranked among the **Top 20 Emerging Cities** (Global Services Magazine). The sector is designated a **government priority for economic development**.

Despite this growth, the HR outsourcing niche remains fragmented with competitors relying on manual processes. A tech-first entrant can capture market share through lower costs, faster service, and superior accuracy.

**Target:** LKR 265,000/month within 6 months, scaling to LKR 3.2M/year.

- Vast supply of skilled, English-speaking labour
- SMEs form the backbone of the economy
- Most SMEs cannot afford full HR departments
- Outsourcing HR is the logical cost-saving measure

## 5. Go-to-Market Strategy

### 5.1 Phase 1: Pre-Launch (Month 1)
- Register business (sole proprietorship)
- Build website + client portal
- Develop payroll automation engine
- Set up LinkedIn company page
- Identify first 5 target SMEs through network

### 5.2 Phase 2: Pilot (Months 2-3)
- Offer free payroll processing to 1-2 friendly businesses
- Collect feedback, refine processes
- Document SOPs
- Publish case studies

### 5.3 Phase 3: Growth (Months 4-6)
- LinkedIn content strategy — HR tips, compliance updates
- Direct outreach to Hayleys vendors/partners
- Google My Business listing
- Target: 4-5 paying clients

### 5.4 Phase 4: Scale (Months 7-12)
- Hire first employee (operations/onboarding)
- Add recruitment and compliance services
- Partner with a labour lawyer for compliance packages
- Target: 8+ clients, LKR 250,000+/month
